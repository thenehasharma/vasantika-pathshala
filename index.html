Our Journey

A small beginning, a divine mission

Vāsantikā Pathshala began during the challenging days of COVID with just 12 students, through three hours of evening tuition. With dedication, faith and continuous service, the strength grew to 70 students within two years.

The journey was not easy. Managing two schools, long working hours and numerous challenges took a toll on health and resources. With the merciful guidance and support of HG Pradyumna Priya Prabhuji, both schools were eventually brought together under one roof.

In November last year, through his kindness and support for infrastructure renovation and staff salaries, a new chapter began. From just 27 students, we have now grown to 140 enrolled students, a milestone of faith, perseverance and collective service.

Our trust, Vāsantikā Pathshala, was named by HH Bhakti Padma Sourabh Pracharak Swami Maharaj, whose blessings remain a source of inspiration.

  1. During COVID

    A lamp lit in the darkest days

    When schools across the country shut their doors, twelve children gathered every evening for three hours of tuition. There was no building and no funding, only a teacher's faith that no child should stop learning.

  2. Two years later

    Twelve became seventy

    Word spread from family to family. Parents saw their children grow in knowledge and in character, and within two years seventy students were coming to learn.

  3. The hardest seva

    Two schools, fourteen hours a day

    An offer came to serve as principal of a school 10 km away. From 6:30 in the morning until 7:30 at night, the seva continued across both schools, through exhaustion, failing health and even attacks by monkeys on the way. Still, the children were never left alone.

  4. A merciful turn

    Brought together under one roof

    Meeting HG Pradyumna Priya Prabhuji changed everything. With his guidance, two autos were hired to bring the children, and both schools became one family.

  5. A year of testing

    When the numbers fell

    For many reasons, student strength dropped to just 27. Days still ran from 6:30 am to 4:30 pm, and health kept declining. It would have been easy to give up. We chose to keep serving.

  6. November 2025

    A new beginning

    Prabhuji mercifully agreed to support the renovation of our premises and the salaries of our teachers. For the first time, the school had a stable foundation to build on.

  7. Today

    From 27 to 140 children in one year

    Our trust is now registered as Vāsantikā Pathshala, a name given by HH Bhakti Padma Sourabh Pracharak Swami Maharaj. Every morning, 140 children chant, study, eat prasadam and grow together. This is only the beginning, and the next chapter can be written with you.

Our Mission

Every child fed, taught and lovingly guided towards Kṛṣṇa

Many children in Vraja grow up without good schooling, a nourishing meal or someone to teach them who they truly are. We exist so that every child who walks through our gate receives all three.

A school can make a child literate. We want to make each child wise, kind and capable: a good student, a good human being and a sincere devotee who can one day stand on their own feet and serve society.
